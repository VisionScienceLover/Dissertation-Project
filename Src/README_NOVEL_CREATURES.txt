PART A: NOVEL-CREATURE VISUAL 2-BACK

Eight fixed grayscale imaginary creature stimuli are drawn in Python.
Each identity repeats exactly for match trials; no line/dot stimuli remain.
The first two trials of each block are unscored.
RIGHT mouse = same identity as two trials earlier. LEFT mouse = different.
Immediate repeats are excluded. Balanced 2-back matches/nonmatches.
Blue/green border alternates to cue the two interleaved streams.
Existing asteroid gameplay and distractor conditions are preserved.
CSV data is saved under part_a_data with 2back-creatures in filename.

To run: RUN_PART_A.bat

NOTE: Stimuli have not been psychometrically normed or tested in a Windows
graphical session. Pilot-test appearance, timing, response recording, and
perceptual discriminability before collecting participant data.
